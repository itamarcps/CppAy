## Runtime and development dependencies

- **Qt** is dynamically linked and retains its applicable commercial/open-source
  and third-party licenses. The Windows package includes upstream license texts
  and the SDK's bill of materials under `licenses/`.
- **FFmpeg** runtime DLLs supplied by the official Qt Windows SDK retain their
  LGPL and third-party notices in the packaged Qt Multimedia SBOM.
- **MinGW GCC** runtime libraries retain GPL terms with the GCC Runtime Library
  Exception; **MinGW-w64** retains its upstream runtime notices.
- **NumPy** is a development/test dependency and is not included in the player.
- Python, Pascal/FPC and SDK downloads are development tools, not player synthesis
  dependencies. Their binaries and downloaded archives are excluded from Git.

Exact Windows versions, source URLs and notices are documented in
`packaging/windows/licenses/DEPENDENCIES.txt` and
`packaging/windows/dependencies.lock.json`. The packager copies the original
project license, AY_Emul notice and dependency notices into release packages.

Unreviewed reference archives, extracted source collections, third-party app
screenshots/video, machine-specific reports and generated audio are also excluded
from Git. The public suite and release packages do not depend on them.
